Things to Do in Cambodia

Top Picks

A curated selection of Cambodia's most iconic and unforgettable experiences.

Angkor Wat

Watch the sunrise slowly reveal the world's most iconic temple in golden light.

Koh Rong

Relax on soft white sands and swim in clear turquoise waters on a tropical island.

Phnom Kulen Waterfall

Cool off in refreshing jungle waterfalls surrounded by nature.

Kep Crab

Enjoy fresh crab cooked with Kampot pepper right by the sea.

Culture & History

Step into Cambodia's past through its royal heritage, art, and powerful history.

Royal Palace

Walk through elegant royal grounds and visit the famous Silver Pagoda.

National Museum of Cambodia

See ancient Khmer sculptures and artifacts in a beautiful traditional setting.

Choeung Ek Killing Fields

Learn about Cambodia's history through a meaningful and emotional visit.

Wat Phnom

Visit the peaceful hilltop temple that marks the birthplace of Phnom Penh.

Nature

Escape the city and reconnect with Cambodia's wild and natural beauty.

Khnorng Phsar

Hike the mountain and enjoy camping in the heart of nature.

Phnom Kulen Mountain

Hike scenic trails and discover sacred carvings and hidden waterfall.

Chi Phat Ecotourism

Experience jungle trekking, wildlife, and eco-friendly adventures.

Cardamom Mountains

Explore vast rainforests, rivers, and remote landscapes.

Temples

Discover ancient temples where history, spirituality, and architecture come together.

Angkor Wat

Walk through grand corridors and reflective pools at sunrise.

Bayon Temple

See the famous stone faces watching from every direction.

Ta Prohm Temple

Explore ruins wrapped in giant tree roots deep in the jungle.

Phnom Bakheng Temple

Climb up for a stunning sunset view over the Angkor area.

Islands

Slow down and enjoy island life with beaches, clear water, and ocean views.

Koh Rong

Enjoy lively beaches, clear water, and a mix of relaxation and fun.

Koh Rong Sanloem

Unwind on quiet beaches with calm, crystal-clear seas.

Koh Tonsay

Relax in hammocks and enjoy simple island life with fresh seafood.

Koh Oknha Tei

Visit silk villages and experience traditional weaving culture.

Food

Taste authentic Khmer flavors, from street food to local specialties.

Amok

Try Cambodia's signature coconut curry steamed in banana leaves.

Num Banhchok

Enjoy a fresh noodle breakfast with herbs and light fish broth.

Kep Crab

Taste famous crab dishes cooked with Kampot pepper.

Num Ansom

Sample traditional sticky rice cakes filled with pork or banana.

Nightlife

Experience Cambodia after dark with food, markets, and city views.

Street Food Areas

Try grilled snacks and local bites in busy night streets.

Bassac Lane, Phnom Penh

Explore a vibrant alley filled with trendy bars, craft cocktails, and live music.

Rooftop Bars

Have a drink while overlooking the Phnom Penh skyline.

Pub Street, Siem Reap

Explore lively bars, music, and crowds in Siem Reap.

Unique Experiences

Discover moments you won't find anywhere else in the world.

Kampong Phluk Floating Village

Take a boat through stilted homes and daily life on the water.

Mekong River Night Cruise

See Phnom Penh lit up at night while enjoying a relaxing river cruise.

Battambang Bamboo Train

Ride a simple wooden cart through scenic countryside.

Phnom Sampeau

Watch thousands of bats fly out at sunset in a natural spectacle.
