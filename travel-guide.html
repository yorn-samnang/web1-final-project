Best Time to Visit

Choose the season that fits your trip

Cambodia feels different in the dry and rainy seasons. Choose the one that matches your travel style, whether you want temple visits, island time, or green countryside views.

Dry Season

Best for temples, beaches, islands, and city walks

Dry season is ideal for visitors who want bright days, easier transport, and comfortable sightseeing across Cambodia.

  • Great for Angkor Wat and temple visits
  • Better island and ferry conditions
  • Comfortable for walking in cities

Rainy Season

Best for green scenery and a calmer atmosphere

Rainy season can still be beautiful for travelers who prefer lush landscapes, softer light, and fewer crowds in some places.

  • Beautiful countryside views
  • Often quieter in some destinations
  • Pack an umbrella and plan flexibly

Transportation

Get around Cambodia with confidence

Use local transport for city rides and combine it with longer-distance options for bigger trips across the country.

Tuk-tuk

Best for short city rides, markets, food stops, and flexible sightseeing.

  • Very useful in Phnom Penh and Siem Reap
  • Great for short distances
  • Apps help with easier pricing

Bus

Budget-friendly and useful for traveling between cities and provinces.

  • Good for intercity travel
  • Useful for lower-cost trips
  • Keep valuables with you

Ferry

Important for island routes and coastal travel, especially near Sihanoukville.

  • Useful for island access
  • Weather can affect timing
  • Arrive early before departure

Plane

Best for saving time and reaching more destinations during shorter trips.

  • Fast for longer routes
  • Helpful for short itineraries
  • Check baggage rules first

Popular Ride Apps

Useful apps for tuk-tuk and city transport

These apps are practical for travelers and make short city rides much easier.

PassApp

PassApp is a Cambodia-based ride-hailing service that is widely used for tuk-tuks and local transportation in major cities.

  • Popular for tuk-tuk rides
  • Useful for local city travel
  • Easy pricing for tourists

Grab KH

Grab is a well-known Southeast Asian ride app that offers convenient transport options and fixed-fare booking in urban areas.

  • Helpful for visitors
  • Fixed-fare convenience
  • Useful in city areas

TADA

TADA is another ride-hailing option for travelers who want flexible transport around Cambodian cities and busy urban districts.

  • Good city travel option
  • Alternative ride platform
  • Simple app-based booking

Currency & Payment

Carry some cash, but expect QR payment too

Cash

Culture & Etiquette

Respect local customs and enjoy a smoother trip

Understanding local etiquette helps travelers make a good impression and feel more comfortable when visiting temples, markets, homes, and public places in Cambodia.

Temple Respect

When visiting temples, dress modestly by covering shoulders and knees. Speak softly, avoid climbing on sacred structures, and follow local signs or guidance.

  • Wear respectful clothing
  • Remove hats in sacred areas
  • Keep noise low and be mindful

Everyday Courtesy

A polite attitude goes a long way. Smile, greet people kindly, and avoid touching someone’s head or pointing your feet toward people or sacred objects.

  • Be calm and friendly
  • Respect personal and sacred space
  • Use polite behavior in public places

Dress Modestly

Especially important at temples, pagodas, and religious sites.

Ask Before Taking Photos

It is respectful to ask before photographing monks, locals, or private spaces.

Be Gentle in Communication

Speaking politely and staying calm is appreciated in everyday interactions.

A comforting noodle soup that works well for breakfast or a lighter meal.

Fresh rice noodles with herbs and light curry sauce that feels refreshing and local.

Travel Tips

Useful things to know before you go

These practical tips help visitors stay safe, comfortable, and prepared while traveling in Cambodia.

Safety

Use trusted ride apps when possible, keep your belongings secure, and stay aware in crowded tourist areas. Save your hotel location on your phone before going out.

Currency & QR Payment

Many places now accept QR payment, especially in cities. It is still smart to carry small cash too.

Weather

Cambodia can be hot and humid, so light clothing, sunscreen, drinking water, and flexible daily planning are very helpful.
