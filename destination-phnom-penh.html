Phnom Penh

Cambodia's vibrant and dynamic capital sits at the confluence of the Mekong and Tonle Sap rivers. A city where royal grandeur, painful history, bustling markets, and a thriving modern scene all coexist in one unforgettable place.

Best For History, Culture, Food & City Life
Region Central Cambodia
Type Capital City

About Phnom Penh

Cambodia's beating heart

Phnom Penh has grown into one of Southeast Asia's most fascinating capitals. The city offers a compelling mix of royal architecture, riverside walks, historic landmarks, lively markets, and a thriving café and restaurant culture. Whether you are here for a day or a week, there is always more to discover.

The city is compact enough to explore by tuk-tuk or on foot, with most of the major landmarks concentrated near the river. Ride apps like PassApp and Grab make getting around easy and affordable.

Quick Facts

  • Province Type Capital City
  • Best Time to Visit November – April (dry season)
  • Getting There Phnom Penh International Airport or bus routes from major cities
  • Getting Around Tuk-tuk, PassApp, Grab, walking along the riverside
  • Currency Riel (KHR) & USD; QR payment widely accepted

Places to Visit

Landmarks & local spots in Phnom Penh

From iconic royal sites to vibrant local markets, these are the places that make Phnom Penh worth exploring.

Historic Site

Royal Palace & Silver Pagoda

The stunning royal compound on the riverfront is home to the glittering Silver Pagoda, the Throne Hall, and beautifully maintained grounds. One of Phnom Penh's most iconic sights.

Allow 1–2 hours Riverside area
Museum

National Museum of Cambodia

A beautiful terracotta building housing the world's largest collection of Khmer art and artifacts, including incredible stone sculptures from the Angkor period spanning over a thousand years of history.

Allow 1–1.5 hours Riverside area
Landmarks

Wat Phnom

The sacred hilltop temple that gave the city its name. Sitting atop a small but prominent hill, it is an important religious site surrounded by a peaceful park that is popular with locals and visitors alike.

Allow 30–60 min Daun Penh area
Waterfront

Sisowath Quay (Riverside)

The lively riverside promenade stretching along the Tonle Sap is great for a sunset stroll, with cafés, restaurants, and open-air seating looking out over the water. A popular spot for both locals and visitors.

Best at sunset Riverside / Daun Penh
Memorial

Toul Sleng Genocide Museum

A sobering and essential part of understanding Cambodia's recent history. The former S-21 prison preserves the memory of the Khmer Rouge era through documentation, photographs, and survivor testimonies.

Allow 1.5–2 hours Boeung Keng Kang
Market

Central Market (Phsar Thmey)

An impressive 1930s art deco building that still functions as a busy local market. Inside you will find gold and jewelry stalls, clothing, electronics, fresh produce, and street food vendors all under one grand dome.

Allow 30–60 min Daun Penh district

Local Tips

Good to know before visiting Phnom Penh

Getting Around

Use PassApp or Grab for tuk-tuk rides around the city. The riverside area and BKK1 are very walkable on cooler mornings.

Dress Respectfully

Bring a light scarf when visiting the Royal Palace or Wat Phnom — covering shoulders and knees is expected at religious and royal sites.

Best Time to Go

November to February offers the most comfortable weather for exploring the city on foot and visiting outdoor landmarks.
